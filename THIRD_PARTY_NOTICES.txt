Dependency and distribution scope
---------------------------------
The locked Rust root/Desktop graphs and the npm lockfile were reviewed for
the Windows two-EXE distribution. These entries highlight important directly
distributed dependencies, not a complete transitive copyright inventory.
Other applicable dependency copyright/license notices must be honored.
Exact resolved packages are tracked in Cargo.lock,
apps/desktop/src-tauri/Cargo.lock, and apps/desktop/package-lock.json.

npm also locks lightningcss 1.33.0 and platform-specific lightningcss
binaries as MPL-2.0. They are frontend build-time tools, not separate
components of the released two-EXE payload. Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime
is supplied by the Windows system and is not bundled by Serial Tool.
